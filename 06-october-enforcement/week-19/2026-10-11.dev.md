# Day 130 - 2026-10-11: findings were correct and repeated, but no actor held write authority for the fix

Dev-log form. One pattern in three instances, plus one human counter-example.

The unifying failure mode of the day: a detector produced an accurate, repeatable finding with a concrete fix address, and the set of actors able to apply the fix was empty (by profile), unnamed (by convention), or reached too late (by message layout).

Position on the line. Day 128: true at write time, stale at read time (time axis). Day 129: true for one consumer, false for its twin (space axis).

Day 130: known and located, but unowned (authority axis). Not a repeat of "instrument lies green" (Days 108-111): the instruments were truthful.

Scale of the day: 14 distinct live sessions, 15 calls, 256 robotic, 3 retrospectives, 22 vault commits, 47 config commits, 1 commit to this book, 4 decisions, 6 research orders.

---

## 0. The shape of the defect

A finding `F` with fix address `L` is emitted by actor `D`. The owner set `O(L)` = actors with write authority on `L` and a trigger to act. Observed: `O(L) = {}` (profile forbids writes), or `O(L) = {"first live session"}` (no identity, no counter), or `O(L) = {human}` with the blocker placed last in a long message.

Required contract: **a finding is emitted with a named owner who holds write authority on its fix address; a queue of fixes without an appointed unloader is accounted as debt.** If the owner is a human, the blocker is line one.
