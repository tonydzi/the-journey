## 🏆 Main story: eight instruments with no right to say "I do not know"

### Case one: the verdict had three values instead of four

**What it is.** The foreign-repository selector judged the niche from a one-line description. Verdict values: our topic, adjacent, off.

**What we found.** An empty description with no topics produced an affirmative "off". Fifty three universe rows had an empty description, and forty three of them went to "off" with no evidence at all.

**Root.** The verdict was typed as levels of interest. The axis "how much input do I have" did not exist in it, so an absence of evidence fell to the default.

**What was fixed.** A fourth value was added. The input was declared as an explicit list of evidence fields. Evidence gathering lives inside the door rather than at the caller.

**The counter-defect in the first fix.** The first version gave a deciding vote to the thread title, which we choose ourselves. The thread is now judged on its own axis and can only confirm a verdict, never assign one.

**Measurements.** Test fifty seven of fifty seven, with eleven failing before the fix. Mutants killed nine of nine, the eighth surviving at first. Drift across one thousand eight hundred and fifty universe rows: zero divergences.

Seventy seven rows had nearly moved to "adjacent" silently.

### Case two: a silent cap printed as the size of the world

**What it is.** An instrument walked the universe and printed a count of finds. The count read as "that is all of them".

**What we found.** The sample cap was set as a default value in argument parsing. The instrument honestly stopped at it and never said so in a single line.

**Why it survived.** The default's blind spot swallowed the founding cases themselves. The breakage lived under four different class names, and the "after the third" threshold arrived under none of them. Honestly the family holds six cases starting September 3, while one or two were visible.

**A separate layer of absurdity.** Sixty four verification instruments sat outside version control: an ignore rule hid the linters themselves from git. In git there were zero.

**What was fixed.** A dedicated linter for silent caps, a family-to-door map inside the breakage counter, and the ignore rule corrected.

**Measurements.** One hundred and eighty two finds across four classes: thirty two, thirty two, twenty five and ninety three. Coverage ninety nine point nine per cent. Test grid thirty four checks, sixteen mutants.

### Case three: "cannot judge twenty of ninety three"

**What it is.** The statement "the test is isolated from production data" was treated as a property of tests. It had never been verified.

**What we found.** Of ninety three foreign tests, sixteen write to production, fifty seven are clean, and **about twenty the instrument cannot judge**.

**Why that line matters more than the first two numbers.** The first edition of the measurement produced a different, understated number, because three holes in the instrument itself had not yet been closed. The line "cannot judge twenty" is the only thing separating a measurement from an invention.

**Root.** The ways to substitute a write path are endless: environment variables, home directory, a constant, a default argument value. The instrument was catching methods instead of the consequence.

**What was fixed.** A new guard judges the fact of a write outside the temporary directory rather than the method of path substitution. Default mode is block.

**And what was not fixed.** Sixteen guilty tests were left as they are. Named individually.

**Measurements.** Guard sixty one of sixty one, self-test eighteen of eighteen, mutants killed eighteen of eighteen. The family lived under three class names, so the threshold was understated rather than overstated.

### Case four: a denominator that included the impossible

**What it is.** Several instruments computed a ratio over a denominator that included the forbidden or the unreachable. The ratio came out handsome.

**What we found.** Sixty three requesting repositories, and five of them have zero foreign open pull requests. There is nothing there to review, and the denominator did not know it.

**The key number of the case.** The batch version of the instrument had thirteen per cent coverage: sixty answers out of sixty nine were "I do not know". On a per-repository probe, coverage is one hundred per cent.

**Root.** No shared contract existed saying "a denominator contains only the permitted and the possible". Each author remembered the boundaries from memory and forgot one each time.

**What was fixed.** The contract moved into a shared module along with a sweeper that walks the code looking for division by a name. Three cases of four were closed, and the parcel is signed for eight nodes.

**About the detector itself.** The first version returned two thousand seven hundred and eighty places across six hundred and forty two files out of nine hundred and fifty four, which is sixty seven per cent of the codebase. That is not a finding, that is a flooder. Narrowed to one hundred and seventy four places in one hundred and two files.

**Measurements.** Seventeen checks, twenty six mutations reddening. The fourth dated case of the family, caught in passing during the repair.

### Case eight: the count of our own breakages read the length of a tuple

**What it is.** The self-healing instrument warns when a breakage class has become systemic. The threshold is three dated cases.

**Why this is worse than it looks.** This is the instrument that decides whether to build a mechanism. It lied in both directions: overstating here, and understating in cases three and two.

### What follows from this

The day's rule: **a verdict must have a value for "I do not know", and every instrument report must print the line "cannot judge N of M, reason".**

Four consequences, paid for on this day.

First. The default when evidence is absent is the verdict the instrument issues most often, and it is usually the most convenient one. If the default is convenient, it will lie in your favour.

Second. A silent sample cap turns an instrument into a generator of false "we walked it all". The cap belongs in the report even when it is not reached.

Third. A denominator contains only the permitted and the possible. Otherwise the ratio is handsome and means nothing.

Fourth. A breakage family is counted by the defect, not by the name the case was filed under. Otherwise the "after the third" threshold arrives not when it is needed but when the wording happens to match.

🤖 **Mike:** And a fifth, which I propose we treat as an honesty check. If an instrument has never once said "I do not know", it is not careful. It is blind and unaware of it.

## 🧒 What we understood today

Any robot judge needs an "I do not know" button. If it has none, it presses whichever one is under its finger, and that is usually the convenient one.

If an instrument has never once said "cannot judge", it is not being careful. It simply cannot say it.

When you compute a ratio, first throw out of the denominator everything that is impossible in principle. Otherwise the ratio comes out handsome and says nothing.

And if you count your own errors by their name rather than their substance, they will never reach three. There will be five of them under five names, and each will stay a "first case".

## 👶 In the simplest possible words

Imagine you are judging whether the biscuits in a closed box are tasty.

You cannot open it. And you only have two cards: "tasty" and "not tasty".

You hold up "not tasty", because you have to hold up something. Everyone assumes you tried one.

All day long we were making the third card: "the box is closed, I did not try one".
