# Day 144 - 2026-10-25: counter is correct under its own definition, the question uses another

Dev-log form.

Defect: counter `C` computes `count(x | def_C(x))`. The question needs `count(x | def_Q(x))`. Some `x` with `def_Q(x) and not def_C(x)` exists and is exactly the case that matters. `C` is accurate; the answer is wrong.

Position on the line. 141: green receipt over a dead output. 142: true reading expires before the click.

143: verdict from one layer (wrong drawer). 144: right drawer, wrong definition.

## 0. Contract

Before trusting a count, read the instrument's definition and locate the target case inside it. Lints declare their blind zone (thresholds, formats). Limits count by event (submit date), not by current status.

Classifier check order is part of the definition: most specific/blocking signal first. Freshness evidence must come from the consumer side, not from a field written by the producer. "Prepared" and "executed" are separate counters; user approval is not execution evidence.

## 1. Speech draft: "two papers"

Problem: draft said "two"; source has 3 (3 distinct DOIs). Value taken from previous-session context; 5th case of the family.

Cause: `outbound_fact_lint.py` threshold = two-digit numbers; numbers in words and single digits out of scope. `have.py` matches by file name; missed a master copy under another name.

Side readings: two instruments on one family: 4 vs 288. Own surface counts 28 vs real 22.

Open: threshold change and content-based search not confirmed in digest.

Pattern: a lint's threshold is a declared blind zone; small counts (people, papers, submissions) live there.

## 2. Limit 4 held 5

Problem: employer disabled its separate jobs host; form embedded in site. Robot judged liveness by page text, closed 21 live rows; 22 reopened.

Cause: per-employer limit 4, actual 5. An application with a submit date later got status `closed`; counter counted open rows only.

Solution: 10 fixes. Liveness from platform API, not page text. `_sent_pair` counts `closed` with submit date.

Related (separate retro): limit 4/180 broken by inter-node race, total 5 at limit 4. Same event or two: not established.

Pattern: limit counts events (submit date), not current status.

## 3. Limit banner filed as field error

1071 descriptions without the limit needle.

Solution: limit detected before field errors; stops with expiry; auto-close at 60 days.

Open: auto-stop proven only by a test on a temporary journal; no live trigger in digest.

Pattern: in first-match classifiers, order = definition; blocking signals first.

## 4. 50 sessions prepared, 0 continued

Pattern: prepared != executed; approval != proof.
