# Day 140 - 2026-10-21: two correct guards sum to the failure each prevents, the sum has no owner

Dev-log form.

Defect: guard `A` (mass-delete tripwire) refuses operation `O` (full vault backup). Guard `B` (once-per-day maintenance gate) counts the refused attempt as the day's run of `O`. Every later writer gets "already ran today" and writes with no rollback point; `A` and `B` are each correct, their composition removes the backup both exist to protect.

Position on the line. 138: rule in words loses to a door at the point of action.

139: instrument with empty input reports nothing, nothing reads as green. 140: each guard is right alone, the sum is orphaned.

Secondary: work finished, consumer stuck (login wall, unscheduled consumer). "Lost" sessions mostly healthy (limit cutoff, reused slot, false batch); disk shows closed work with named tails.

## 0. Contract

A quota gate counts successes, not attempts. A refusing guard must signal its refusal to any gate that meters the same operation.

Every pair of guards on one operation has a named owner of their composition. Before declaring a session lost, read the disk: limit message, slot reuse, shared-prefix false batch.
