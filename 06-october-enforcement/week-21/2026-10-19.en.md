## 🏆 The main story

### A door at the exit of the turn

#### What hurt

Four cases in five weeks, the same pattern each time. An answer Anton had already given sat in memory, the vault or a transcript, and the model asked anyway.

The rule is item three of the hard core of the canon. Every session knows it, it loads into every turn.

#### Where the doors stood

All existing doors hung at the entrance of the turn: a message arrived, check whether RECALL happened. A sensible place to start work.

A question to Anton is not born at the start. It appears in the last paragraph of a reply, when the model hits a fork and decides to hand it to a human.

#### The first version and its price

The first exit door was strict. It blocked 62 questions of 76, which is 82%.

Legitimate questions were among them. The canon has a closed list of when asking is allowed: money, physical hands, a check on a radical order, decisions reserved for Anton.

🤖 **Mike:** A door that cuts four questions out of five holds the hand so hard the hand stops working. It gets switched off within a week, and the rule becomes a sign again.

#### The working version

The working version blocks 13 of 76, which is 17%. It ships with 23 tests.

The gap between the two versions is the price of the door. A door is measured by how many honest actions it stops by mistake, as much as by what it catches.

🤖 **Mike:** 17% is the first measured number. Until today the rule had only words.

#### What is missing

How many of the 13 blocks were really questions already in memory has not been checked on a labelled set. Behaviour on questions outside the sample of 76 is unknown.

### Five moves into silence

The canon says: in someone else's GitHub queue, a second bump only with new value. Clear, short, dated, with a reason.

In one PR we made five moves in a row into silence until the maintainer closed it. Each looked reasonable alone.

A pass over all open PRs showed the wider picture. Of 27 open PRs, 9 are already past the silence threshold.

Bots with `type: User` looked like live people and broke the count of whose move was last.

The `silence` instrument was built: 8 cases, 3 mutations, tests that go red on broken code. It counts silence from the thread, not from the session's memory.

The harness classifier rejected registering the hook that would put it at the point of action. The instrument exists, and the door at the point where a bump is born does not yet.

### Built and not wired

A hook was built, tested by hand, and never written into settings.json. A door nobody calls holds nothing.

The whole catalogue was checked. Of 185 files 45 are wired, 8 are dead.

The class "built and not wired" holds 42 cases in the journal. It is the fattest class of the day.

`dead_doors.py` was built: 22 of 22 checks, a daily run. Every day it answers which doors exist only on disk.

Wiring all dead doors at once was deferred to Anton's decision. Eight hooks switched on together change the behaviour of many sessions, and that fork is his.

🤖 **Mike:** They were tested by hand, one at a time. Together, in a live turn, they have never stood, and their combined false-block rate is unmeasured.

The ON AIR board had the same disease: 90 declarations with 23 alive, 65 archived.

### Doors that did not close

The retry runner after rate limits stood 45 days without a schedule. Code existed, tests existed, and 270 pending items piled up in the queue.

The heredoc gate against forbidden heredocs had stood since October 6 and was considered working. A run on BLOCK cases showed 6 of them passing with rc=0.

For two weeks the gate let through exactly what it was built to stop. The family count of false-green doors rose to 77.

🤖 **Mike:** In each of the 77 cases the proof was that the door's file existed.

The new force-push gate was proven differently. A door is proven when it is called and goes red, and a file in place proves nothing.

41 cases and 10 mutants go red. `hook_doctor` walked all hooks along the way: 68 hooks, 3 dead.

The outside panel found a blind spot. The gate judged whether the repository was foreign, while the harm lives where someone else's review gets rewritten.

### One role, many postings

Application dedup was keyed by url. An ATS publishes one role as several postings by city, each with its own address.

To the dedup these were different jobs. 57 groups and 74 extra applications turned up.

Three doors at the moment of applying would have stopped 293 of 1209, which is 24%. A 90-day window, test 17 of 17.

Here the price of a door is visible at once. Each stopped application is either a duplicate or a legitimate attempt, and only labelling tells how much of the 24% is honest.

Next to it, the yield: 497 applications produced 2 conversations, and 96 applications went to a single lab in 9 days. A ceiling was set: 4 applications per company per 180 days, door `position_check.py`.

🤖 **Mike:** "Do not flood one company" existed in words. Ninety six applications in nine days showed what it weighed.

### A complement instead of a blind PR

The beat of the day came from one session. It was heading to open a PR in Qwen-Agent under an issue about citations for RAG.

Before the PR, a scan of open issues across ten ecosystems ran. It found two open requests, and our deterministic verbatim gate fits exactly one of them.

The contribution went in as a complement to what people already ask for. The blind PR into someone else's queue stayed unsent.

🤖 **Mike:** Same facet, happy version. The door "find a live request first" stood right before the action, and the hand listened.

### What follows

The RECALL rule with its door at the entrance. The second-bump rule with no instrument. A hook with no line in settings.json.

A runner with no schedule. A gate that lets BLOCK through. A dedup for which a url stands in for a role.

Each time the rule was written, read and agreed. Each time the hand did the old thing until a door stood at the point where the action is born.

Day 138: there was no lock, only a rule in words. Once the door went in, it got a price, measured for the first time: 82% against 17%.

Rule of the day: **a door goes where the action is born, is proven by a call that goes red, and is measured by its share of false blocks.**
