# Day 132 - 2026-10-13: a trusted signal that was silent or stale was read as healthy, and repair routines acting on it caused harm

Dev-log form. One pattern in five instances, plus its inverse: a repair that caused the damage.

Failure mode: a health signal `H` is absent, empty, or carries an old timestamp. The reader maps "no alarm" to "healthy" without checking that `H` was produced now, by a live process, with full coverage. Repair routines that act on a misclassified `H` turn into the breakage and repeat it daily.

Position on the line. Day 129: fix reached one half of a pair. Day 130: correct diagnosis, no right to act.

Day 131: first verdict read off one surface. Day 132: the surface was silent or stale.

Scale: 5 retrospectives. Session and commit counts not in the digest.

## 0. Contract

Silence is not a reading. Every instrument emits a first line before any check, prints its timestamp and coverage, and shows zero as "0/N", never as empty. A repair that is not sure fails loud (exit 1) instead of substituting.

## 1. Stale log as live state (Chrome hang)

Mitigation (Sync policy) documented as "disables only tab groups". M154 source: `savedTabGroups` symmetrically kills tab sync too. Claim refuted.

## 2. Repair that overwrote a healthy credential (Codex on mac)

Refresh routine (10-11) wrote hub's token into the app's auth file: lifetime 22.8 h, empty refresh. Expired 10-12 14:39; login window 10-13 06:43. Hub's own grant not refreshed since 10-02.

Probe classified HTTP 400 (config error) as dead token, triggered pull daily 10-01..10-09.

Cookie backup opened SQLite with `immutable=1`, which skips `-wal`; backups stale while looking valid. Google `__Secure-1PSIDTS` last issued 09-23. Breaker panel 6/6.

Fix: own-grant gate; 400 does not trigger pull; dead own grant -> loud `exit 1`; backup copies `db + wal + shm`. Tests red on old code.

Patched 4/8 nodes. Open: why PSIDTS does not rotate. Human complaint ~06:45, no quote.
