# Day 134 - 2026-10-15: guards and checks built from the same substance as their subject

Dev-log form. One defect class, 15 retrospectives.

Shape: check `C` guards subject `S`, and `C` depends on a resource, method, clock or author shared with `S`. When that shared thing fails, `C` fails with `S`, or `C` agrees with `S` by construction.

Position on the line. 131: first verdict from one surface. 132: silent or stale signal read as "fine".

133: fix did not reach the consumer, count from one slice.

134: the check existed, was fresh and delivered, and still could not see, because it shared the failure mode.

## 0. Contract

A guard must not share an exhaustible resource (subscription bucket, vendor quota, account, token) with its subject. A behavioural claim needs a behavioural test. Cross-node time is UTC.

"Done" requires a write call in the transcript. Hardware facts come from the host.
