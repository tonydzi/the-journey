# Day 131 - 2026-10-12: a first verdict read off one surface was accepted as evidence until a second pass by another method refuted it

Dev-log form. One pattern in ten instances, plus one human counter-example in both directions.

Failure mode: a verdict about state `S` is derived from a proxy surface `P` (file size, tool reply, exception branch, gate status, aggregate score, a human's statement). `P` is correlated with `S` but not identical. The verdict is reported before any second method reads `S` directly.

Position on the line. Day 129: a fix reached one consumer of a pair. Day 130: a correct diagnosis had no owner.

Day 131: the first diagnosis itself was wrong.

Scale: 9 distinct live sessions, 18 calls, 216 robotic, 27 retrospectives, 17 vault commits, 48 config commits, 0 commits to this book, 3 decisions, 6 research orders.

## 0. Contract

A first verdict is a hypothesis. It becomes a claim only after a second pass using a different method that reads the target state directly. Proxy surfaces print what they are, not what was asked.
