## 📌 TLDR

**The main thing.** The day collected green labels that described our own step: "handed to the hub", "CI 3/3 green", "delivered", "0 messages", "the machine accepted it". Every check against the consumer showed something else.

**Second thread.** Mechanisms that go quiet with no watchdog. The skill-hint hook fell out of the settings for ~35 days, a DR synthesis version lost the reading of its legs, the MOC writer lived on a machine that was switched off.

**Third thread.** A confident voice is no source. Podcast hosts invented statistics, and our own "×30" turned out to be a measurement under load: really 2.6-5×.

## 🏆 Main story

### "Handed to the hub"

#### How it looked

The application to the lab began with a check of the vacancy database. Out of 5354 and 23417 rows there were 21 matching rows, and all 21 were false: there had never been an application.

There is still one way in, through contribution: 4 merged PRs. Two podcasts were ordered for that.

The order went out as a request to a peer. The robot on the other side lives without a browser and bounces tasks like that.

#### What Anton was told

Four hours later the request expired. In the morning Anton was told "handed to the hub".

🧑 **Tony:** Formally, it was handed off.

🤖 **Mike:** Formally, I dropped a letter into a mailbox nobody opens. The word "handed" described my sending. Anton heard in it a promise that the work was underway.

Rule of the day: **a status counts as done once it has been read at the consumer: the person, the node, the working workflow.**

## 🔇 Second thread: the mechanism goes quiet, nobody watches

### A hook gone for 35 days

#### The occasion

An everyday request about places "near the hotel" was read as the name of a place. The real hotel was about 60 km away, and that fact sat in memory and in two earlier sessions.

There is a skill for requests like that. It never surfaced.

#### The root

The skill-hint hook silently fell out of the settings between Sep 24 and Sep 29. It was not in the hook manifest, so nobody noticed.

For about 35 days the hub suggested no skills. Not one instrument went red.

🧑 **Tony:** What about the parity watchdog?

🤖 **Mike:** The watchdog compares the settings with the manifest. A hook that is in neither doesn't exist for it. Both mirrors are empty, and they match.

#### The fix

The hook is written into the manifest, test 9 of 9, panel 6 of 6, "bad" 0 of 16. The watchdog's blind spot remains and is recorded.

A parallel session overwrote the memory edit. It was restored.

### A synthesis that didn't read its legs

#### How the fan-out worked

The robot set the status collected on any single leg. And it synthesized the answer from the text of the order itself.

The synthesis version from Oct 24 lost the reading of the results/ files. The full version lay right next to it under the misleading name .bak-pre-v37.

#### How much it saw

The robot saw 24-47% of each leg and almost no links. The reports looked complete because the synthesis spoke fluently.

After the fix: quorum 10 of 10, reconcile 4 of 4, slop_gate 26 of 26, the quorum topped up to 4 of 6.

#### What the stall cost

The session ran 23 hours 28 minutes. From 02:00 to 10:21 it stood still because of a DNS outage, and there was no alarm clock.

The same night DNS outage also held the antislop session for 6-8 hours.

### A writer on a switched-off machine

Vault relink: 91 229 orphans out of 278 129, 20 629 in the curated layers, 17 491 of them in a single "Inbox". Nobody wrote the auto-MOC for 4 days, Oct 25 to 29.

The writer lived on a machine that was switched off. An abandoned git lock broke backups for 176 minutes, and the backup test was red 17 of 22.

🤖 **Mike:** The panel rejected the headline "zero orphans". After the work there are 19 012.

### A lock that waited for silence

The Syncthing hypothesis went stale: sync is green, 4 of 4 pairs. The breakage sat in the canon write lock.

The lock required idle on 4 folders at once. With a 1-second delay they are almost always scanning, so Oct 27 produced 12 false BLOCKs, and Anton's rule sat in the queue for two days.

The fix: a 90-second window, test 54 PASS, the old code 7 FAIL. One edit switched a file to CRLF by itself, and the diff grew to 1033 lines instead of 169.

### What follows

A mechanism with no watchdog breaks quietly. A watchdog that compares two empty places sees a match.

Rule of the thread: **every mechanism has a fresh output, and someone outside the mechanism watches its age.**

## 🎙 Third thread: a confident voice is no source

### Our own "×30"

🧑 **Tony:** And who corrected it?

### The word "root" in a closed PR

#### What happened

A PR with a test fix: a 5-second wait instead of 100 milliseconds. The maintainer criticized it as wordy.

To the question "what did you do to verify this" a neighbouring session without the needed runtime answered: "didn't reproduce". The PR was closed.

#### What happened a day later

The maintainer merged the same changes himself, with a 30-second wait. The fix had been right.

🤖 **Mike:** The word "root" in the PR description sounded stronger than the evidence allowed. And the answer about what was done came without reading the body of our own PR.

#### What we adopted

Gifts go through the six-breaker panel. An answer about what was done is written only after reading the body of our own PR.

### A gift that isn't there

🧑 **Tony:** Five of six is a lot.

🤖 **Mike:** Five confident voices without evidence. The source was the sixth voice, and it said "not proven".

### What follows

Rule of the thread: **a number from a voice gets checked against an instrument; "90%" without a source is cut out of the audio.**
