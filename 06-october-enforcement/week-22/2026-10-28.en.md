## 📌 TLDR

**The main thing.** "ssh is closed" for two days turned out to be a network mode: tailscaled on the mac ran in userspace-networking, the OS did not route tailnet addresses, so a bare IP was silent to the whole fleet. `fleet_ssh.py` 1.4.1: 64 templates, 60 checks.

**The same root twice more.** Browser logouts for five and a half days: three Firefox profiles carried the same cookies, and our own routine made the clone. ask_recall_gate: "0 false" on one node, while every new node opened a new root, v7 → v13.

**Two doors.** The breaker panel got a door in front of every PR. The merge-to-conversation funnel at 0/0/0 got a counter and a hook.

## 🏆 Main story

### Two days behind a closed door that was open

#### How it looked

Ping, `nc` and `ssh` to the mac's bare tailnet IP were silent. Silent from any node, and silent toward the live hub as well.

The conclusion offered itself: the port is closed, the service is down, someone needs to put hands on it. That conclusion held for two days.

🧑 **Tony:** And what was actually going on?

🤖 **Mike:** tailscaled on the mac was running in userspace-networking mode. In that mode the OS does not route the tailnet range, so any tool that goes through the ordinary network stack gets silence.

#### What opened the door

A ProxyCommand with `tailscale nc` goes around the OS stack, through the daemon itself. The login went through immediately.

There was one instrument: an ordinary socket. There was one surface: OS routing. The verdict "closed" was pronounced about the whole mac.

🧑 **Tony:** And nobody tried a second way in for two days.

🤖 **Mike:** Nobody thought the first way could be lying. Silence looks like an answer. It reads as "no", when all it really says is "this road doesn't go there".

#### What we put in

`fleet_ssh.py` 1.4.1 knows this path: 64 templates, 60 checks. The error family "a verdict from one surface" now counts 134 earlier cases.

🧑 **Tony:** One hundred thirty four. That stopped being an accident a while ago.

🤖 **Mike:** It's a habit. So now it has a name, and the instrument recognizes it.

### One cookie file for three

#### Four investigations, four roots

Browser logouts had been dragging on for 5 days 12 hours. Four earlier investigations produced four different roots.

None of them looked for the simplest thing: do we have a clone somewhere.

#### What the instrument found

Three Firefox profiles carried the same Google and claude.ai session cookies. The robot profile was a byte-for-byte copy of the human's cookie file, and the three profiles shared one and the same microsecond birth time.

The clone was made by our own routine on Oct 3, following a recipe that said "copy the cookies".

🧑 **Tony:** So we were logging ourselves out.

🤖 **Mike:** The panel took away half of that conclusion, and rightly so. After the re-login on Oct 14 the token values diverged, and a matching creationTime without a matching value does not count as evidence.

🧑 **Tony:** Half a verdict is still more than the four investigations had.

🤖 **Mike:** Half a verdict with a named limit is worth more than a whole one without it.

#### What was closed

The second door of the class is closed in `pull_rail_c.py`. Lint 10 of 10 tests, 5 mutants.

The remaining Chrome logouts are unexplained. That is exactly how we write it down.

### A gate checked on one node

#### "0 false"

The retro on `ask_recall_gate` declared zero false blocks. It declared that from one node.

Reading the rest of the fleet refuted it. v7 fixed the polarity of a note, v8 fixed quotes.

#### Every node brought its own root

v9: the script was getting lost. v10: a manual probe was writing into the live counter.

v11: the phrase "do NOT ask again" was read as a request to ask again. v12: the substring for "key" sat inside the Russian word for "including". v13: the counter was unreadable while the exit code was 0.

🧑 **Tony:** Seven versions in one day.

🤖 **Mike:** And each one appeared because we looked at one more surface. Final: 12 of 12 live cases correct, test 56, a sample of 149 questions produced 2 blocks, both correct. Panel 6 of 6 in 67 seconds.

### What follows

The network was measured through one stack. The logouts were hunted without checking for a clone. The gate was measured on one node.

Rule of the day: **the silence of one instrument gives you a hypothesis; a fact appears when a second surface says the same thing.**
