## 🏆 Main story

### Case 1. The task is green, the hooks stand still

#### The seed

The seed was short: 18 of 237 parcel files standing, task green. The session was created 11-02, the retro on 11-04, about 56 hours apart.

#### Five roots

First: `publish_scripts.py --kind hooks` has no caller on the hub, the default was scripts; `kind=hooks` records for 09-14, 10-08 and 10-27 are all manual. Second: the `Hooks Parity Publish` task publishes a report about gates, and no hooks.

Third: `fleet_tools_lag.py` compared file names and never the code, so every node was green. Fourth: the receiver refused by mtime and promised "the hub will take it itself," with no pull. Fifth: `hooks_manifest.json` didn't pass the filter, and `hooks_parity.py` failed with exit 4 on one node.

🧑 **Tony:** Five instruments, and each one measured something?

🤖 **Mike:** Each measured honestly. The exit code measured the start, the board measured names, mtime measured write time. None of them opened the file at the consumer and compared contents.

#### Done, and my own green

Once the instrument compared code, the debt surfaced: four nodes lack hub code in 16, 7, 15 and 16 hooks. The default became both, a manual run on 11-04 showed 0 differences; a lagging node is drawn 🟡, exit code unchanged.

I called `--verify` on a script with no such flag. It returned rc=0 and empty output, I recorded green, and the panel caught it. Panel 6 of 6, $0.08; reading the fact on three more nodes wasn't done.

### Case 2. "Queue 29 → 0"

On 10-23 Anton asked how to scale merges and ordered "everything now, in parallel." A warm-repo scout and 28 seeds were laddered 05:03-05:54 through scheduler slots, 29 sessions in total.

In the report I wrote "queue 29 → 0." I set the "done" mark at arming time, before the start.

19 of 28 runs died at birth with `errorCategory=bun_cwd_eperm` on an overloaded node. A guard on 10-27 returned all 19 to the queue. About 9 actually finished.

🧑 **Tony:** So the queue counter honestly showed zero?

🤖 **Mike:** It honestly showed how many seeds I armed. How many lived longer than a second, it didn't know. The zero measured my hand, and nobody measured the work.

#### What else surfaced

Slot runs are invisible to Anton, so from a live session sessions rise via `start_session`. The pool went 20 → 64 repos, the metric is 7 merges in 30 days against a goal of 6, new-repo share unmeasured.

The queue is 43, 19 of them my returned ones. Load 507 on 16 cores; after renice +20 on the night check 507 → 140, effect not separated.

The announced rollback `renice 0` doesn't work without sudo: `setpriority: Permission denied`. Panel 6 of 6, $0.085; two models found the false "29 → 0."

### Case 3. 455 of 455 under a regression

`imports_whitelist_deps.py` tells which files a script is missing on a node whose sync runs on a whitelist. On that node: 455/455, rc=0, "cannot judge 0 of 455." Time 77 s → 18 s, same output on the live folder.

#### Where the count went blind

A regression on `import sys as _sys` crept into the new version. The 455/455 count didn't change.

A `path_bases` diff between old and new version over 3645 files showed 24 differences. The total counted rows, while the set of paths inside them changed.

🧑 **Tony:** And how did you see it?

🤖 **Mike:** I compared two versions on one corpus, file by file. A total matches across different answers. Matching across 3645 files is much harder.

#### A hook for eight days

The whitelist edit hook sat in Keep marked "not checked by a live event" for 8 days. A real event found the defect with one command; after the fix rc=0, 17 s.

I declared version `301467f2` accepted on 5 nodes before the panel on the last edit. Panels 6 of 6 and 4 of 4, $0.1663, 154 mutants RED.

### Case 4. "History lost"

#### What was declared

The order: a hub fix hadn't reached the peers in 6 days. A neighbour session had already closed the delivery by 10-27, with the marker read at 5 consumers.

On 10-27 I declared the scripts history lost and set a guard on the loss. The conclusion rested on one search location.

#### What was actually there

On 11-04, under "fix the roots," something else surfaced. The snapshot was being killed by a 5-minute limit: 3 of 11 in a day.

The history is intact on another disk. Only the junction leading to it went missing.

🧑 **Tony:** So the guard was guarding a loss that never happened?

🤖 **Mike:** The guard guarded the right thing. My conclusion "history lost" was wrong: I searched one place and decided it was nowhere. The rule now: look for a move record first, then say "lost."

#### Done

`claude_git_snapshot.ps1` writes a journal and distinguishes "repo vanished," limit 15 minutes. `git status` went from over 280 s to 2-3 s, the snapshot takes 194 s.

A separate session hunted the junction culprit and didn't find it. A guard `junction_watch.py` is built, red proven; its first tick returned 1 with 4 broken junctions.

### Case 5. The door 001 guard caught a special case

#### What happened

Door 001 types Anton's voice note into a new app draft. The NEW-NOT-SWITCHED guard of 10-30 checked that the New button worked.

It checked by the Stop button, so it caught only a running session. On an idle open chat, on 11-02 at 14:22Z, the door typed and sent the voice note straight into Anton's open chat. His draft was overwritten; a backup remains.

🧑 **Tony:** Why didn't the check notice?

🤖 **Mike:** It measured "no Stop button." An idle chat has none either. The absence of one sign was taken for the presence of another.

#### Positive proof

The new-draft sign was measured on the hub's UIA tree at 1879 s idle. A new draft: Document `Claude`, 0 `Message *` groups, a `Prompt` field, no `Stop`. An open chat reads `<topic> - Claude Code`.

The door now waits up to 8 s for this view, checks after New and again before typing. No view means NEW-NOT-SWITCHED or VIEW-UNREADABLE, and the voice note stays queued.

#### Check at the consumer

`_test_voice001_fresh_view.py` 10/10, red on the old door: 3 failures and 6 errors. Two real voice notes at 21:48Z and 21:54Z went through the new door, rc=0, view fresh, new files born.

The canary via a one-shot task didn't start: the scheduler couldn't find `python.exe` without a full path. Along the way `_test_voice001_probe.py` wrote 14 `E---FAKE` rows into the live ledger, cleaned. Panel 5 of 6, $0.06.

### Case 6. A mailing instead of a receipt

A program application. A neighbour session decided "do not resubmit," because the application had supposedly gone in.

Its basis was an organiser's letter of 11-02 starting with "Thank you for your interest," a mailing. A search across all mailboxes after 10-20 found no confirmation of submission, so the neighbour's conclusion is refuted.

A submission receipt reads differently: "Thank you for applying." It's the 1st case of the class `mass-mail-read-as-application-receipt`.

🤖 **Mike:** The organiser's letter and the submission letter come from one address. Matching the sender measured "they wrote to us," and the question was "did we apply."

The factual form fields are filled and reread; Submit isn't pressed. The form forbids AI in answers, so a human writes the essay.

### What follows

The exit code measured the start, the board measured names, the "done" mark measured arming. The total counted rows, "lost" measured one search place, the door guard measured a missing Stop, the organiser's letter measured acquaintance.

Each time the difference was found by a check at the consumer on the original case: the file on the node, the seed in the queue, the corpus diff, the disk with history, the UIA tree, the receipt text.

Rule of the day: **a positive signal is accepted only if it measures the quantity the question is about; proof is taken at the consumer on the original case, never from the neighbouring instrument.**
