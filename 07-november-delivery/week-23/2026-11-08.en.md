### Case 3. Three weeks without a shard

### Case 4. Commit 135 GB, working set 508 MB

The hub ran out of Windows commit with 71 GB of physical memory free. An idle embed-server held 135 GB of commit while its working set read 508 MB.

The instrument watched the working set. It was looking in the wrong room.

The root: `ThreadingHTTPServer` creates a thread per connection, and torch with OpenMP builds its own team of workers for each thread and never retires it. Now `encode` goes through one persistent `ThreadPoolExecutor(1)`.

Before: 200 requests gave +9.31 GB and 1087 threads. After: +0.19 GB and 93 threads, and 800 requests reached a plateau. CommitFree went 7.1 → 74.6 GB, and the hub ran the gold 184 itself.
