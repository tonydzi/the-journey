# Week 24 (Nov 9-15) - the week a thing is asked where it came from and who signed that it arrived

> *A fix is only yours when its bytes are yours. A receipt is only true when the consumer wrote it. This week asks both questions of things that were already marked done.*

The week opens with Day 159 (November 9): an automatic rollback on a laptop that took a backup made on the hub, with 85 hub paths in it and none of the local ones, a fix that lived on one node while the hub walked without it, and a night "dream" that wrote lessons nobody had verified under a judge's borrowed name.

Day 160 (November 10) asks the mirror question: a queue, a forecast and a watchdog that stamped "done" at the moment of intent, so the receipt arrived before the fact.

Day 161 (November 11): a verdict that held on a single line. "Three times more honest than Mem0" was one refusal threshold (3 of 22 without it, 10 of 22 with it, at the price of 12 extra false refusals out of 69), and a stop-hook, an exit-code check and a browser watchdog each hung on one hidden setting too.

Day 162 (November 12): the remedy with a knife. A reindex with `--force` wiped a live code index to zero, and the first two versions of the worktree hook could delete a copy holding someone else's work; a test and a second panel caught both, and a door in the code closed them.
