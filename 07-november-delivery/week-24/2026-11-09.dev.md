Defect: an artifact (backup, signature, file version, interpreter, task setting) originates on another node or from another writer, and the consumer accepts it without checking provenance.

## 1. Rollback from the hub backup (ZBook)

## 8. Patterns

P1. Every restored or imported artifact carries its origin node; consumer rejects foreign origin by default.

P2. A signature is taken from the responder, not from the router's intent.

P3. A fingerprint is taken at verification time, not after.

P4. Parity is md5 at each consumer, with "cannot judge K of M" printed.

P5. A merge is guarded by tests for every earlier fix; ship with no red neighbour.

P6. Scheduled jobs pin interpreter and logon mode explicitly; a generic fixer does not touch foreign-owned tasks.
