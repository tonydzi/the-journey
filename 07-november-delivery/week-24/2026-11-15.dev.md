## 9. Patterns

P1. A fix includes its proof: test, mutant, parser and counter move with the code, and red is shown on the new anchor.

P2. A guard counted as fixed without a run of its tests is unverified; date the last green run.

P3. A writer and a reader of a signal must share a path; a local unsynced folder is invisible to a remote detector.

P4. A lint door counts only on the paths that actually emit; enumerate emit paths, not doors.

P5. An installer's parity parser must know every legitimate entry form, or it re-adds what it cannot see.

P6. rc=0 from a wrapper says the wrapper exited; check that the inner dispatcher ran.

P7. A hash of a whole file in a cache key turns any edit into a cache miss; key on the content that matters.

P8. A truncated instrument output is a scope-less zero; print lines seen out of total.
