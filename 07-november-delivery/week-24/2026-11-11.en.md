## 🏆 Main story

### Case 1. Three times more honest, while the threshold stands

The memory test looked good. Recall@5: ours 0.88, Mem0 0.87, Fidelis 0.86, BM25 0.77, so on retrieval everyone is close.

The gap was in honest refusal: 0.45 against 0.14. In the table that read as 10 correct refusals of 22 against 3 of 22 for Mem0 and 4 of 22 for the third entrant.

🧑 **Tony:** Sounds like something to bring to an interview.

🤖 **Mike:** Which is why I went to check. A conclusion that flatters us needs one more piece of evidence on top.

The replay ran over the same 91 saved answers. With the refusal threshold, 10 of 22 correct refusals; without it, 3 of 22; Mem0 also 3.

The threshold has a price: 12 extra false refusals out of 69. The system says "I don't know" more often, including where it knew.

🤖 **Mike:** "Three times" is true in exactly one sense: as the share of refusals on questions that have no answer. The phrase does not travel wider than that.

The claim is now written as a precise formula: the threshold, its effect and its price on one line. A loud number about ourselves came down to one parameter, and we showed it ourselves.

### Case 2. A watchdog opened by an unfamiliar word

The stop hook catches a false "no": a session saying "the file does not exist" without checking. It read any "no" about some other object as "no file" and blocked work.

The watchdog had been fixed with an allowlist of harmless words. An unfamiliar word was not on the list, and the door opened.

🧑 **Tony:** A list of what is allowed, and everything else is allowed too?

🤖 **Mike:** That is how it worked. All of the watchdog's strictness lived in one list.

The list was flipped into a denylist. The block lifts only on a positive sign that the sentence is about another object, and an unfamiliar word no longer opens anything. Test 19 of 19, opponent corpus 60 of 60.

### Case 3. One line between a command and its code

Since 11-04 the nightly sync reported exit=0. The profile was logged out, and the sync was not working.

An exit code is lost by any line between the command and the read of its code. Here that line was `set` inside a `.cmd`, and the watchdog hook knew neither `.cmd` nor substitution.

🤖 **Mike:** A week of green was held up by one housekeeping line. It broke nothing, it just stood in between.

No new watchdog was built. The existing door was widened to recognise the case.

14 mutations went red. The class family has 14 cases since 09-03; a sweep over 27 thousand commands found 35 new refusals.

### Case 4. Red by guess

The watchdog of a browser rail marked it red. The trigger was a restart and silence, with not a single attempt to use it.

🧑 **Tony:** It recorded a failure it never saw?

🤖 **Mike:** It recorded a guess under the word "failure." A quiet rail and a broken rail look the same until you knock on it.

Red is now set only after three or more failures since the last success. Test 35 of 35, 5 nodes on the new version.

### Case 5. The root is boring and elsewhere

Mac16 was slow in the mornings. The nightly brain eval ran on CPU for 6-8 hours, and the night-window lock gated only the start.

At night agents-python used 492%, in the morning 320%, in the day 28%. By the time of SIGSTOP it had gone through 140 of 184 questions.

The first fix was a patch: a hard end at 05:00 UTC. It treated the symptom.

The root turned out to be boring. The hub was already running the same eval, on CUDA, in 370-942 seconds.

🧑 **Tony:** Mac16 spent every night computing what the hub had already computed?

🤖 **Mike:** Yes, and on a stale index: the hub has 108116 chunks, Mac16 a lagging 107131. The launchd job on Mac16 is switched off, not deleted.

Hub figures: title 0.95, body 0.85, bridge 0.43, temporal 1.0.

### Case 6. A fix that did not arrive

The rail-plan lock lost its stop on the eighth move. On 11-04 a tool intake replaced the file with the hub's version under the same name.

The plan was declared in 13 of 32 sessions before the lock, 32 of 35 with it, and 32 of 67 after the swap. The stop branch is back; 57 checks.

MacStudio had no browser counter hook: the rollout never reached the node. Four files and a watchdog agent were installed, and along the way it turned out that Write, Edit and Agent had never reached the dispatcher.

More broadly this is the class "script delivery without an applier." Studio has 221 files against 3309 on the hub, and a live node without a mirror used to count as "unknown."

🤖 **Mike:** Now a live node without a mirror is red. But the detector is fixed on one node, so I do not call the class closed.

The rollout tool, on rollback, said "the file was not there." An unreadable hash was confused with a missing file, and the path carried a literal `%USERPROFILE%`.

A separate EXISTS/ABSENT probe now runs. Old code 53 of 56, new 56 of 56, then 59 of 59; applied on 4 of 8 nodes, 3 offline.

### What follows

A threshold, a list, a `set` line, a rule for red. In each case the whole verdict hung on one setting, and nobody showed that setting next to the verdict.

Rule of the day: **next to a verdict stands the line it hangs on, and that line's price.** "Three times" is written with its threshold, "green" with whatever read the exit code, "red" with its number of attempts.
