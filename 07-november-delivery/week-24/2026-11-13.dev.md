## 6. Patterns

P1. Any "we beat X" first runs with our own addition switched off; the claim's size is what survives.

P2. A threshold-driven headline is a statement about the threshold; say so in the headline.

P3. Publish the comparison where we lose; it is the asset that earned the next stage.

P4. GPU use is proven by nvidia-smi utilisation and VRAM, never by a log word; "faster" and "on GPU" are separate claims.

P5. Count a corpus by the indexer's own rules.

P6. Before enabling a disabled task, read who disabled it; a lock signs its reason where the operator looks.

P7. A counter excludes conflict copies; a detector without an executor is a blind node.
