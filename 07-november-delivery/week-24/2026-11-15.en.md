## 🏆 Main story

### Case 1. A guard whose tests burned red for ten days

`grep -r` over the vault, `_imports` or the 5.9 GB working folder outlived the 120 s ceiling. The empty output was then read as "no".

The bash-grep class has lived in the journal since September 13, seven lines. `msys_find_guard.py` knew only home and `.claude`, and on five heavy roots `judge()` returned None.

🧑 **Tony:** But it was fixed.

🤖 **Mike:** On November 5. After that 15 of 22 tests stayed red for ten days, and nobody ran them.

A second gap turned up: `selfheal.py journal` reopened a closed class whenever "починено:" (fixed:) appeared in `--what`. The family counter ran on the old rule and logged a relapse where there had been a repair.

Fix: roots from `machine.env`, the drive root on Windows only, selfheal patched. Tests 27/27, and red is proven: with `heavy = set()` they fail.

Selfheal 12/12, panel 6/6, a live hook on the hub and on the Mac Studio. The Mac Studio immediately caught my own test.

🤖 **Mike:** On two nodes ssh was refused by key, on the rest the port is closed. They got the `bash-grep-heavy-roots-20261115` package; I did not see it applied there.

### Case 2. A witness writing where nobody looked

On November 13 and 14 the detector reported SILENT on MEMORY.md, although the witness hook was in place. Seventh case of the canon-witness class.

🧑 **Tony:** So the hook wasn't there?

🤖 **Mike:** That was my first thought: "the hook is only on the hub". The hypothesis was false.

The witness wrote into a local `scripts/` folder, which does not sync. The hub detector read only its own counter, and other nodes' records never reached it.

The first request to the nodes went over the bus with no payload. Fix: the witness duplicates its lines into `_canon_witness_<node>.jsonl` next to the canon, the detector reads those files, package `witness-shared-20261115`.

Test 0 failures, 1 failure on the old detector. Not checked on the nodes.

### Case 3. A number with no method in a podcast

Yesterday a NotebookLM podcast said "749404 broken links". The number came from a scanner and the label "broken" was false: 92% of the targets sit in copies outside the vault.

The `outbound_fact_lint.py` door existed, but nobody called it on the "podcast pack" path. Eighth case of the "number without a method" class.

🤖 **Mike:** The door stood in one corridor, and the podcast walked down another.

Fix: the defect kind is cleared only by a `--src` that contains the word "метод:" (method:). The door is built into `teach_nb_setup.sh` and returns exit 5 before the notebook is created.

Tests 48/0, red_proof twice, a live pack rc=5. Open: `material-to-audio` is wired by hand and not rolled out to the nodes.

### Case 4. Mutants on the old anchor

Class pipeline-eats-own-prompt, third breakage. The root was closed on November 14, but the mutant file failed on its anchor after the gate edit.

Red proof D2 had never been pinned to the mutants. The anchor and expected cases were fixed: 6/6 mutants go red on their own set, main test 30/30.

🧑 **Tony:** So now everything gets caught?

🤖 **Mike:** I checked by hand. The mutation "drop `not force_eligible and`" gives 30/30 green; there is no case for 11 Codex with `driver: robot`.

The hole is confirmed and named. Another loose end turned up next to it: `brain_ask.py` indexes the session-card robot's prompts as conversations, and that went to a new session.

### Case 5. A hook entry that silently never entered

SessionStart p50 32 s over 68 starts, UserPromptSubmit p50 6.6 s, Stop p50 7.2 s. Every session waited half a minute before its first word.

`install_hooks.py` wrote 20 guards back in directly. `hooks_parity` knew only `hook_dispatch.py` and not `hook_client`, so the guards inside the new entry looked missing to it.

The entry `cmd.exe /d /c ...hook_client.cmd` does not work under Git Bash. MSYS mangles `/d /c`, cmd reads the hook's JSON as a command and returns rc=0.

🧑 **Tony:** How long did that last?

🤖 **Mike:** Since November 14 the dispatcher silently did not run. And my removal of the duplicates left sessions without guards for 15 minutes.

Fix: entry = `hook_client.exe EV`, 6 rules moved into the dispatcher. SessionStart 18-29 s became 1.4-2.1 s, after the edits p50 1.33 s.

Afterwards a neighbour fixed two dispatchers on one port, let in by SO_REUSEADDR. The force-push guard turned out to be broken by its test while the code was fine: 0/43, red probe 26/43.

The author of the 12 disabled rules is not identified.

### Case 6. The night pipeline and my cache key

TX Daily Core broke for the fifth night of five. The 120-minute budget was counted from the start of the row loop, while the inventory, adapters and the 7 GB write ran on top of it.

Two regex gates of ~3.5 h each were replaced by a pool of 12 processes: 14 and 17 minutes. The lock used to hold up to 4 h after a killed process; now pid liveness is checked.

🤖 **Mike:** My mistake: editing `tx_publish.py` changed the hash of the whole file, and that hash is part of the `scrub_hash` cache key. About 4 h of runs went to recomputation; I rolled back from backup.

Tail 48.5 min rc=0, release 58048 rows, tests 3/3 and 10/10. A full night run is not proven, the night of 11-16 will show it, and the pid-reuse race is not closed.

### Case 7. Zeros without maps, continued

Signal-gaps was blind for the third time. The audit looked only at tasks with a non-zero today, while the hole in the map is permanent.

`selfheal._run` cut output to 400 characters. Out of 568 tasks, 9 lines reached the audit.

🧑 **Tony:** So, yesterday's zero without a map.

🤖 **Mike:** The same one. The instrument saw nine lines and honestly reported on nine.

Fix: a `--static` mode (4 s instead of 18+ minutes), `_run(limit=None)`, four robots got a map entry and a crash-guard. `_test_crash_guard` went red on CRM-Backup-Pull, then ALL PASS.

Static mode yields 61 candidates. Panel 6/6 made me soften "FIXED" to "partial".

Coverage Map Nightly: the watchdog painted the task by LastTaskResult while the robot was alive, rc=0, a 3 min 22 s run. Only the 11-11 reboot explains the red nights; the root is not proven.

I did RECALL late. A neighbour session had already disabled the task as a duplicate of Housekeeping Daily, and my manual watchdog run without `-NoHeal` added 11 extra escalate lines.

### What follows

A guard with red tests, a witness in an unsynced folder, a door off the podcast path, mutants on an old anchor, an installer that did not know the new entry. Each time the code was edited correctly, and the check kept looking at the previous version.

Rule of the day: **a fix is closed when its red proof is re-anchored to the new code and goes red on breakage.** The test, the mutant, the parser and the family counter are part of the fix.

## 🧒 In plain words

You fix a leaking tap and leave the old water meter in place. The meter still reads the old pipe, so it either shows a leak that is gone or misses a new one.

Today the meters were tests, mutants, an installer and a counter. Now a fix counts only when its meter is moved to the new pipe and shown to react to a leak.
