## 7. Patterns

P1. A zero report carries its scope: surface, method, read count of total. No scope, no acceptance.

P2. The brief is the root when N independent sessions share one gap.

P3. "Not found" from a single-location probe is evidence about that location; check registrations, exports, other roots.

P4. Refute the natural hypothesis by measurement before acting on it (54 sessions vs two antivirus scans).

P5. A pause by complaint records the quote and the reason in the task; lifting it names what was fixed.

P6. A reviewer's stop counts when the author receives it; delivery is part of the review.

P7. A rail or reviewer name in a report is a claim with the same burden as the conclusion.
