## 🏆 Main story

### Case 1. A board that asked itself

#### "bb accounting is broken"

`fleet_token_board.py` gathers token spend from every node. On the Studio it took the Studio for the hub, and the real hub was never polled. That was the thing everyone called "bb accounting is broken".

The fix is short: the hub was added to the peer list over ssh, and the local node was removed from it. The hub became 3 644 M tokens instead of 0.19, which is 29% of the Claude weight.

🧑 **Tony:** And the total?

🤖 **Mike:** 5 709 sessions, 6 nodes, 24 377 M tokens over 7 days. The Codex bucket is at 91% until 29.11, and the Studio gave 46% of that.

#### A bucket that showed zero

The Claude bucket on the board sat at 0%, from a reading 514 minutes old. After a manual get_usage feed it showed 4%.

The root: the `plan_budget.py --post` watcher on the Studio is not wired. An automatic check refused the change to settings.json, and that is left to Tony.

#### The second check

I checked the board against the session files, by a different method. I set the refutation threshold in advance: 10%.

Hub 1294 against 1255, 3%. ZBook 442 against 431, 2%. Studio 727 against 667, 8%.

🤖 **Mike:** All three under the threshold, the board held. I did not run the outside panel, and that is written down.

### Case 2. A move that reread itself

#### Histories in the vault

Mac16, Codex. Program histories moved inside the vault, and the old paths became links. 15 paths were checked on three marks: samefile, inside_vault and symlink, all true.

The archive of 13 source proofs was reread, 0 mismatches. Claude live: 31 313 -> 31 322 records, 0 inode losses.

Qwen: 969 files with no mismatches. Antigravity: 396 databases, 396 WAL and 396 SHM.

#### An open database

The root: an open SQLite cannot be copied like a folder. A rename followed by a link creates a race, and the copy loses a record still sitting in the WAL.

The fix was an atomic directory swap on the same disk.

🧑 **Tony:** So the copy would have looked whole.

🤖 **Mike:** It would. The file count would match, and the last record would be gone.

#### The book on Mac16

The guard: a missing root is rejected before the old book is replaced. Installed on 5 nodes of 6, Studio UNKNOWN.

### Case 3. A check that saw 6 of 9

#### One list, one runner

The Studio, Opus 5.5. The /self-check skill keeps one list of checks and one runner. It went from 22 checks to 26, and each new one grew out of a real breakage: machine.env 06.11, the vault 13.11, the bus 17.11.

#### Windows

On its first run the hub gave 24 of 26. Two bash checks do not work on Windows, so the instrument went red because of itself.

They were replaced with the envpath type, and it became 26 of 26. All 6 nodes gave 26 of 26, exit 0, each through its own separate request.

#### Two failures

First: a shared "first one takes it" request would have closed on the answer of a single node. A fleet check needs a separate request per node.

Second: deploy_register refused the rollout through the probe_only_apply gate. The register was not written, and a human decides.

🧑 **Tony:** So "6 of 6" sounds better than it is.

🤖 **Mike:** In fact it is 6 of 9. Two nodes have been silent for about 1281 hours, and one has no signals.

The panel of 6 of 6, for $0.081, made me put that right into the report. Three remarks were accepted: the runner prints the list fingerprint sha=fc39de7320bb, "6/6" means 6 of 9 nodes, and red probes are split by type, 5 RED and 3 OK.

One remark was rejected: that the register was closed by going around it.

### Case 4. Records from the future

#### Fifty five on top

ZBook. Hot memory put 55 records with future dates on top: the message time from the md file came out later than the write time of the file itself.

The root: the md time is written by the node's clock and read as world time. Records from the future are now 0.

#### A foreign copy against a live file

`_holds()` now decides this way: a foreign copy beats a live local file only if it is longer and written later. The first version broke continuing a session on another node through a headless resume call.

Tests caught two failures before release.

#### Junk titles
