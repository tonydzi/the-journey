## 12. Patterns

P1. Reconnaissance is measured by a walked door. Before a fan-out of N, walk one unit end to end to a human answer.

P2. A launch is not a start. After `start_session`, verify linked sessions exist before reporting.

P3. A selection ruler taken from a reference must be run on the reference.

P4. A gate's rejection count measures volume, not accuracy; without ground truth, do not claim the gate pays for itself.

P5. A zero in our own lane is first searched for in our gates and queues.

P6. Zero calls from a connector without login is not a demand signal.

P7. A healer must raise what it kills, or it must not kill.

P8. "Not found" must print the homes walked (N of N).

P9. ACK absence is not apply absence; prove apply by measurement on the node.

P10. Published is not delivered; check the share config and the file at the receiver.

P11. A permission another node judges must be written next to the object, in the synced place, bounded in time.
