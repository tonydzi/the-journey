## 8. Patterns

P1. A setting has as many homes as there are readers of config. Enumerate homes (clients, machines, blocks, copies) before the fix; verify in each.

P2. A fast result in the repaired home is not fleet evidence. Measure the unrepaired homes.

P3. A target is not a measurement. p95 needs a sample, not two runs.

P4. SSH config is first-match. A managed block must be placed first, or a stale manual block wins silently; `ssh -G` shows the effective value.

P5. Retention is per home. Data kept "forever" by policy may already be gone in a home with a 30-day default.

P6. A receiver of a self-deleting source needs `ignoreDelete`, or one home's deletion propagates.

P7. Old `sync-conflict` copies are readers' load. Digest instead of delete.

P8. A snapshot answers about its date. Verdicts on live state read the live store.

P9. A continuation reads the parent's tail and neighbours before acting.

P10. A hook launched by a quoted path in PowerShell needs `&`; a hook must drain stdin even when its target is absent.
